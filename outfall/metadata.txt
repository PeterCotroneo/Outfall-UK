about=Outfall UK loads every designated bathing water in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland onto your map, coloured by its official annual quality classification (Excellent, Good, Sufficient, Poor) or — where published — today's short-term pollution-risk forecast. It can also show LIVE storm overflows (sewage spills): a second layer of the storm overflows that are discharging right now, or discharged recently, from every English and Welsh water company plus Scottish Water, updated near-real-time (within about an hour) from the Water UK "Stream" feeds behind the National Storm Overflow Hub. All data comes straight from the regulators and water companies, free and keyless: the Environment Agency (England) and Natural Resources Wales via the DEFRA platform, SEPA (Scotland), DAERA (Northern Ireland), and the water companies' storm-overflow services. Toggle nations, switch between rating and pollution-risk colouring, and click any site or overflow for detail. Built on the same engine as the Wake (marine vessels), Contrail (aircraft) and Zenith (satellites) live-tracking plugins.
